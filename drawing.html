Drawing Tips I've Learned

Start with Basics

Master fundamental shapes and proportions before diving into complex artwork. Simple foundations lead to stronger pieces.

Practice Consistently

Drawing daily, even for just 15 minutes, is more valuable than sporadic long sessions. Consistency builds muscle memory.

Embrace Your Style

Don't copy others entirely—let your unique perspective shine through. Your style is what makes your art special.

Study From Real Life

Observing the real world helps you understand anatomy, lighting, and textures that make art more believable.

Don't Fear Mistakes

Mistakes are learning opportunities. Some of my best discoveries came from happy accidents.

Share Your Work

Getting feedback from others helps you grow and builds confidence in your abilities.
