What Music Means to Me

Music is my constant companion, whether I'm happy, sad, or in need of motivation. I listen to music every single day—on walks, while drawing, or just relaxing.

My playlist is a whirlwind of pop, lo-fi, K-pop, and catchy video game soundtracks. If I discover a new favorite, it's on repeat for weeks! Sometimes, my artsy ideas *start* with a song, and music gives me energy during study marathons or those inevitable rainy-day moods.

My Musical Journey

I started building playlists in middle school and now, music shapes nearly every activity in my life. I love sharing tracks with friends and exploring concert livestreams.

Sometimes, just putting on headphones and closing my eyes for a gorgeous melody is the best way to relax and recharge. Music helps me focus and sparks creative daydreams—every song tells a story.

How Music Inspires My Art

The connection between music and visual art is undeniable for me. A single song can spark an entire illustration concept. When I'm stuck on a drawing, the right playlist can unlock my creativity. Video game soundtracks especially fuel my imagination.
